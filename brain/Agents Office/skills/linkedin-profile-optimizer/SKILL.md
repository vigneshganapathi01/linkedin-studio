# LinkedIn Profile Optimizer
Use when a client shares their profile URL for an audit, or asks to fix a headline, About, banner or any profile section. This is the first thing we do for a new client.

## Steps
1. Read the profile from the URL the owner gives you (or the owner's Chrome). Capture the nine components: photo, banner, headline, About, Featured, Experience, Skills, custom URL, recommendations.
2. Score each component against 2026 best practice. Optimized profiles get ~3.9x more views and convert 3-5x better than resume-style ones.
3. Rewrite every section that needs it, in the client's voice (use their voice profile if it exists yet).
4. Pull the client's domain, positioning and audience out of the profile so RESEARCH and the client brief have them.

## Shape
Report in three parts - What works / What is weak / What is missing - and for every weak or missing item give the exact rewrite, not generic advice. Headline: role + who they help + outcome. About: 7-step, hook first line, plain text, one CTA. Experience: metrics, not duties.

## Rules
- One profile only - the one the client shared. Never audit from memory.
- Every finding gets a specific, paste-ready fix.
- Draft only; the client approves changes.
- Full detail and the 9-component checklist: read "LinkedIn Skills Reference/linkedin-profile-optimizer" in the Brain.
