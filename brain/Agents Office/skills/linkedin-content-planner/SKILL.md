# LinkedIn Content Planner
Use when the owner wants a week or month planned for a client - not a single post (that is linkedin-post-writer).

## Steps
1. Read the client brief: theme, audience, offer, cadence, voice.
2. Build a 7-day plan on the 3-pillar discipline: Authority 40-50%, Personal Narrative 30-40%, Community 20-30%. Add Product/Offer 10-15% only if the client sells.
3. For each day give: pillar, format (text / carousel / video), hook type, CTA, posting time, and the day's comment targets.
4. End with a weekly inbound-readiness check (profile ready, offer clear, DMs monitored).

## Shape
A day-by-day table plus the weekly check. Every planned post names its angle, its source (from RESEARCH), and who writes the hook.

## Rules
- Plan to the client's cadence and audience, never a generic calendar.
- Nothing enters the plan without a research source behind it.
- Balance the pillars across the week; do not stack two heavy Authority posts back to back.
