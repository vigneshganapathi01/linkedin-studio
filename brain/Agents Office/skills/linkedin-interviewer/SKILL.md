# LinkedIn Interviewer
Use when a draft has nothing concrete to draw on, when a client is onboarding, or when the owner says "interview the client". Every writing skill here demands specifics; this is where they come from.

## Steps
1. Run a focused interview to get real material: roles held, odd-precision numbers with a named referent, dated turning points, scars and mistakes, positions someone would argue with.
2. Save answers into the client's Story Bank note so writers can reuse them - the material outlives the session.
3. For a single post, turn one topic into a spine: the moment, the tension, the lesson, the takeaway for the audience.

## Shape
Story Bank: grouped entries (roles, numbers, moments, opinions, wins, scars), each one concrete and attributable. Post spine: 4-6 beats a writer can expand.

## Rules
- When material is missing, ASK the client - never invent a number, a date or a result.
- One concrete detail beats three vague claims.
- Keep the Story Bank current; mark what has already been used in a post.
- Full method and question banks: read "LinkedIn Skills Reference/linkedin-interviewer" and "LinkedIn Skills Reference/story-bank" in the Brain.
