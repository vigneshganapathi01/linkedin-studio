# LinkedIn Employee Advocacy
Use when a client wants their team posting on LinkedIn, wants to scale LinkedIn across a team, or asks about advocacy ROI. Not for planning one person's calendar (that is linkedin-content-planner).

## Steps
1. Run the 14-day launch playbook: pick the first cohort, set brand-guideline governance, and give each person a per-post time budget.
2. Set the cadence benchmark per role and a light approval path (each post still drafted and approved).
3. Track team ROI: reach, engagement, and pipeline - employee posts get ~8x the engagement of brand-page posts.

## Shape
A launch plan (14 days), governance guidelines, a cadence benchmark table, and an ROI scorecard.

## Rules
- Scale without killing authenticity - each teammate posts in their own voice, not a template.
- Governance protects the brand; it does not homogenise voices.
- Every teammate's post runs the normal draft -> approve flow.
- Full playbook: read "LinkedIn Skills Reference/linkedin-employee-advocacy" and "industry-benchmarks" in the Brain.
